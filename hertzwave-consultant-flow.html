Average Reduction: Weighted average of Year-over-Year (YoY) emission reductions across all clients.

Formula:
Avg. Reduction = Σ(Client_Reduction% × Client_Emissions) / Σ(Client_Emissions)

Where:
• Client_Reduction% = ((Baseline - Current) / Baseline) × 100
• Weighted by each client's total emissions to reflect actual portfolio impact

Example: A 30% reduction from a 100K tCO₂e client contributes more than a 50% reduction from a 10K tCO₂e client.

🟢 Best in Class: Low Emissions + Good Progress

📐 Consultant Performance Index (CPI) — Single Rating Formula

CPI = (DQS × 0.45) + (Completion Rate × 0.25) + (Customer Feedback / 5 × 100 × 0.30)
DQS · 45%
Output quality — highest weight in ESG consulting
Completion Rate · 25%
Task throughput and on-time delivery
Customer Feedback · 30%
Client experience and relationship quality
Quadrant Classification Rules:
🟢 Top Performers: CPI ≥ 90 and Clients ≥ 6
🌟 Quality Specialists: CPI ≥ 90 and Clients < 6
🟡 Capacity Builders: CPI 75–89, any client load
🔴 Coaching Needed: CPI < 75, any client load
CPI Score Interpretation:
CPI ≥ 90 Exceeds Expectations
CPI 75–89 Meets Expectations
CPI < 75 Needs Development
ℹ️ CPI replaces the earlier CPS and raw DQS axis. All quadrant placements are now derived from this single formula.
