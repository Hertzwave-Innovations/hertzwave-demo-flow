📋 Evidence Guidelines

Evidence files are linked to specific ESG categories and visible to assigned Auditor roles. Follow these guidelines to ensure audit readiness:

  • Upload original source documents (not edited copies)
  • Files must be clearly dated and identifiable
  • Utility bills should show meter readings and billing period
  • Lab reports must include accreditation details
  • Use descriptive file names before uploading — e.g. electricity-bill-Q3-FY25.pdf
  • Tag to the correct reporting period — auditors filter by this
  • Multi-page PDFs preferred over multiple image files

Average Reduction: Weighted average of Year-over-Year (YoY) emission reductions across all clients.

Formula:
Avg. Reduction = Σ(Client_Reduction% × Client_Emissions) / Σ(Client_Emissions)

Where:
• Client_Reduction% = ((Baseline - Current) / Baseline) × 100
• Weighted by each client's total emissions to reflect actual portfolio impact

Example: A 30% reduction from a 100K tCO₂e client contributes more than a 50% reduction from a 10K tCO₂e client.
