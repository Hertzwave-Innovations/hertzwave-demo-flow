💡 Understanding the Carbon Credits Marketplace

What is the Marketplace?
The carbon credits marketplace connects buyers with verified offset projects. Credits represent one tonne of CO₂ reduced or removed, certified by independent standards like Verra, Gold Standard, or Climate Action Reserve.
How to Choose Credits
Consider project type alignment with your values, vintage year (newer is often preferred), certification standard quality, price competitiveness, co-benefits (biodiversity, community impact), and geographic relevance to your operations or stakeholders.
Credit Standards Explained
Gold Standard emphasizes sustainable development co-benefits. Verra VCS is the largest registry with diverse projects. Climate Action Reserve focuses on North American projects. All ensure rigorous verification and additionality.
Vintage Year Importance
Vintage indicates when the emission reduction occurred. Newer vintages (2023-2024) are typically more valuable as they're more relevant for current compliance and demonstrate recent climate action. Some programs require vintages within specific windows.
Quality Ratings
Premium/Gold credits have additional certifications (e.g., Climate, Community & Biodiversity Standards), strong co-benefits, and rigorous verification. Verified credits meet baseline standards. All listed credits are independently validated and tradeable.
Pricing Factors
Credit prices vary based on project type, vintage year, certification standard, co-benefits quality, market demand, and scarcity. Nature-based solutions (forests) typically cost more than industrial projects (renewable energy) due to co-benefits and permanence considerations.
Purchase Process
Click "Purchase" to specify quantity, review project details, confirm pricing, and complete transaction. Credits transfer to your inventory within 48-72 hours pending registry confirmation. You receive documentation including serial numbers and certificates.
Co-Benefits Value
Co-benefits are positive impacts beyond carbon reduction: biodiversity protection, job creation, community health, water security, or sustainable development. Strong co-benefits enhance your ESG story and stakeholder appeal but typically command higher prices.
Due Diligence Tips
Review project documentation for verification reports, methodologies, and additionality proof. Check registry status to ensure credits are valid and not retired. Verify project continues operating and hasn't faced controversies. Consider third-party ratings if available.
Bulk Purchasing
Large purchases (10,000+ credits) may qualify for volume discounts and dedicated account management. Contact sellers directly for custom pricing, delivery schedules, and portfolio packages that meet specific compliance or sustainability commitments.
Credit Delivery Timeline
After purchase, credits typically transfer within 2-3 business days. The process includes payment confirmation, registry transfer initiation, seller approval, and final registry update. You'll receive serial numbers and can track status in your inventory.
Avoiding Greenwashing
Ensure credits are additional (wouldn't happen without carbon finance), permanent (for removal projects), not double-counted, and from projects with robust monitoring. Use recognized standards and transparently communicate your offsetting strategy to stakeholders.
