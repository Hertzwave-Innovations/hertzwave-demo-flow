Carbon market compliance › What is required & why
India's carbon compliance landscape
Three regulatory regimes — PAT came first in 2012, CCTS is being introduced sector-by-sector and coexists with PAT during the transition period, and CBAM is the EU border mechanism that uses verified output from both through its own separate calculation layer.
Key abbreviations
⚡
PAT
Perform Achieve & Trade
BEE · 2012 onward · Cycle VII active
Energy efficiency regime. Designated Consumers receive a Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) target in TOE/unit output. Over-performers earn tradeable ESCerts. CCTS is being introduced sector-by-sector — PAT and CCTS coexist during the transition period.
DC thresholds — sector-specific, not universal
Cement: >30,000 TOE/year
Steel: >50,000 TOE/year
Other sectors: sector-specific — confirm via BEE registration
Metric: SEC (TOE/unit)Cert: ESCert
🏭
CCTS
Carbon Credit Trading Scheme
MoEFCC / BEE · Notified Oct 2025 + Jan 2026
GHG-intensity regime introduced sector-by-sector alongside PAT. Below-target facilities earn tradeable CCCs. Above-target must purchase or pay penalties under Carbon Credit Trading Rules 2023. Coexists with PAT for non-notified sectors.
Notified sectors
Oct 2025: Cement, Aluminium (primary), Chlor-alkali, Pulp & Paper
Jan 2026: Secondary aluminium, Petroleum refinery, Petrochemicals, Textiles
Anticipated: Iron & Steel (CCTS notification pending, PAT Cycle VII active)
Metric: GHG intensity (tCO₂e/unit)Cert: CCC
🇪🇺
CBAM
Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism
EU Reg 2023/956 · Definitive phase from Jan 2026
Separate calculation layer at product/CN-code level. Legal declarant is your EU customer. Indian exporters supply verified embedded-emissions data. CBAM is not a copy of domestic CCTS/PAT — it has its own boundary, methodology, and verifier requirements.
Covered goods (CN code level)
Cement (CN 2523) · Steel (CN 7206–7314) · Aluminium (CN 7601+)
Fertilisers (CN 31xx) · Hydrogen (CN 2804.10) · Electricity
Declarant: EU customer (annual)Indian exporter: data supplier

Step 1 of 5
Product boundary lock

Step 2 of 5
Methodology selection
Default values vs actual embedded emissions per CN code. Justify any departure. Lock to EU/UK regulation version in force at filing time.
Step 3 of 5
Per-shipment calculation
Quantity x verified intensity = embedded tCO2e. CCTS/PAT intensity used as input — recalculated at product level, not copied wholesale.
Step 4 of 5
CBAM-specific independent verification
Separate from domestic ACVA. Verifier accepted under EU/UK CBAM regulation — not automatically same entity or standard. Issues CBAM-specific verification statement.
Step 5 of 5
Declarant confirmation & dispatch
EU/UK customer receives and countersigns the supplier emissions package. TerraCAP stores EU and UK evidence packs separately — they are different regulatory records.

CBAM Supplier Compliance Module
A fully separate compliance engine at product/CN-code level. EU CBAM and UK CBAM are managed as independent modules — different legal instruments, different authorities, different evidence packs, different carbon prices. Neither is a copy of the domestic PAT/CCTS calculation.
⚠
Anti-contamination lock active. CBAM calculations use their own product-level boundary, methodology, and emission factors. PAT/CCTS facility-level factors cannot be silently reused — any carry-over requires explicit review and approval in Step 1 below.
ℹ
Your role: data supplier, not declarant. Your EU/UK customer is the authorised CBAM declarant who files the annual declaration and surrenders certificates. You supply verified embedded-emissions data per shipment. TerraCAP generates separate packages for EU and UK customers.
