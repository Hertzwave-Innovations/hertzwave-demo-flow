💡 Understanding Portfolio Management

What is Portfolio Management?
Portfolio management helps you track, analyze, and optimize all your carbon credit holdings in one centralized view. It provides visibility into your credit diversity, value, risk exposure, and overall offsetting capacity.
Portfolio Diversification
Diversifying across different credit types (renewable energy, forestry, etc.) and vintages reduces risk. If one project faces verification issues or market price drops, other holdings maintain value and compliance capability.
Credit Vintage
Vintage refers to the year the emission reduction or removal occurred. Newer vintages (2023-2024) are typically more valuable as they're more relevant for current compliance periods and demonstrate recent climate action.
Portfolio Value Tracking
Credit prices fluctuate based on market demand, regulatory changes, and project quality. Tracking portfolio value helps you understand your asset position and make informed decisions about when to purchase, hold, or retire credits.
Active vs. Retired Holdings
Active credits are available for retirement or trading. Once credits are retired for compliance or voluntary offsetting, they're permanently removed from circulation and cannot be reused, ensuring the integrity of your offset claims.
Portfolio Optimization
Regular portfolio analysis helps optimize your credit mix for cost-effectiveness, compliance needs, and stakeholder preferences. Consider factors like credit quality, co-benefits (biodiversity, social impact), and alignment with your ESG goals.
