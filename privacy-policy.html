2. Data We Collect and Process

2.1 Carbon Accounting Data
  • Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions data and calculations
  • Energy consumption records and utility bills
  • Supplier and vendor carbon footprint information
  • Facility location and operational data
  • Transportation and logistics emissions data
  • Raw materials and product lifecycle data
  • Waste management and disposal records
2.2 Business Information
  • Company profile and organizational structure
  • Employee headcount and operational metrics
  • Financial data relevant to carbon intensity calculations
  • Regulatory compliance status and reporting history
  • Industry classification and business activities
  • Supply chain and vendor network information
2.3 Technical and Platform Data

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Contract Performance

We process your data to provide core platform services, generate ESG reports, and fulfill our contractual obligations for carbon accounting and compliance management.

Legitimate Interest

Platform optimization, security monitoring, fraud prevention, and improving our carbon accounting methodologies based on aggregated usage patterns.

Legal Obligation

Compliance with regulatory requirements including BRSR reporting, CBAM regulations, CCTS guidelines, and other applicable ESG disclosure mandates.

Consent

Advanced analytics, benchmarking features, third-party integrations, and marketing communications require your explicit consent.

4. Data Sharing and Transfers

🛡️ Your Data Security Commitment

We never sell your carbon accounting data. All sharing is limited to specific purposes with appropriate safeguards and your control.

Regulatory Bodies

As required for BRSR compliance, CBAM reporting, and other regulatory submissions. You maintain control over what data is submitted and when.

Authorized Consultants

ESG consultants and auditors you specifically authorize can access relevant portions of your data through controlled, audited interfaces.

Cloud Infrastructure Partners

AWS/Azure cloud services with data processing agreements and appropriate security measures. Data remains in your designated geographical region.

International Transfers

For global enterprise clients, we use Standard Contractual Clauses and ensure adequate protection levels for cross-border data transfers.

5. Data Security Measures

🔒 Enterprise-Grade Security

HWCAP employs multiple layers of security to protect your sensitive carbon accounting and business data.

  • End-to-end encryption for data in transit and at rest (AES-256)
  • Multi-factor authentication and role-based access control
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • ISO 27001 aligned security management framework
  • Dedicated tenant isolation for enterprise clients
  • Automated backup and disaster recovery procedures
  • 24/7 security monitoring and incident response
  • Regular employee security training and background checks

6. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and GDPR (where applicable), you have the following rights:

Right of Access

Retrieve and review your carbon accounting data, emissions calculations, and platform usage information.

Right of Correction

Update and correct inaccurate emissions data, facility information, or business details in real-time through the platform.

Right of Erasure

Delete your account and associated data, subject to regulatory retention requirements for compliance reporting.

Right of Data Portability

Export your carbon accounting data in standard formats (CSV, Excel, PDF reports) for migration or backup purposes.

Right to Object

Opt-out of non-essential data processing including marketing communications and advanced analytics features.

📧 Exercise Your Rights

Contact us at privacy@hertzwave.com or use the privacy controls in your HWCAP dashboard. We respond to requests within 30 days.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data based on regulatory requirements and business needs:

  • Active Accounts: Data retained for service duration + 3 years
  • Carbon Accounting Data: 7 years minimum (Companies Act 2013)
  • Regulatory Submissions: 10 years (BRSR requirements)
  • Aggregated Insights: Indefinitely in anonymized form
  • Marketing Data: Until consent withdrawal + 30 days
  • Security Logs: 3 years for security monitoring
