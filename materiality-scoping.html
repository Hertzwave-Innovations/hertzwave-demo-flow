Scoping & Rationale

Step 4

Assign materiality status and document threshold method, rationale, and evidence for each record. Threshold method is set once at assessment level; all records inherit it.

Assessment-Level Threshold Settings
Set once for this cycle — inherited by all records. Use the Edit button to update.
The threshold method defines the rule your organisation used to decide what counts as "material" for this cycle — e.g. any topic rated High by the external assessor on both impact and financial dimensions. Setting this once at assessment level means every topic in the register uses the same yardstick, which is what auditors and CSRD require. Individual topics can still have override justifications where the general rule does not apply.

A — Framework Mapping
Each material topic is matched to the reporting standards that cover it. Only material topics appear here — go back to Step 4 to mark topics as Material first.
Framework mapping answers the question: for each topic that is material to us, which specific reporting standards apply? For example, if "GHG Emissions" is material, it maps to ESRS E1, GRI 305, and BRSR Core. The confidence tag tells you how strong the match is — Explicit means the standard directly covers this topic, Related means it is closely connected, Recommended means TerraCAP suggests it based on sector context. These mappings then tell the Compliance Mapper which disclosures to prioritise for your company.

C — Approval Sign-Off
Two levels of approval are required before this assessment is locked and platform feeds activate
Why two levels? Level 1 (Batch Approval) is about the scoping decisions themselves — the person who decided which topics are material submits batches for their designated reviewer to check. Level 2 (Final Assessment Approval) is a senior sign-off on the complete assessment as a whole — confirming that the evidence trail, completeness review, and all batch decisions together constitute a defensible, audit-ready materiality assessment. Only after Level 2 approval does TerraCAP lock the assessment and activate the platform feeds and compliance disclosures.
1 Batch Approval — Decision Owner submits, Designated Reviewer approves
Who does what: The person who made the scoping decisions (Decision Owner) groups topics into batches and submits them for review. The Designated Reviewer checks the rationale and evidence, then approves or sends back with comments. This is about checking the individual topic decisions.

Who does what: Once all batches are approved, the Assessment Owner submits the full assessment for final sign-off. D — Export
All exports are available at any time, marked Draft until Final Approval is granted
You can export at any point during the process — you do not need to wait for approvals. However, exports before Final Approval carry a Draft watermark, which tells auditors and third parties that the assessment is still in progress. After Final Approval, the watermark is removed and the export is the locked, audit-ready version. The XLSX export is the most complete — it includes every field, every rationale, source references, and the full evidence trail. The PDF is a readable one-page summary for board or stakeholder communication.
