⚙ What is a Designated Consumer (DC)?
Any industrial plant consuming energy above BEE's notified threshold for its sector is a Designated Consumer and must comply with PAT (and eventually CCTS). For Iron & Steel, the threshold is 30,000 TOE per year. At 0.32 MTPA with a blast furnace, SLRM Hospet is well above this threshold at an estimated 84,000+ TOE/year. Facilities below the threshold are non-DC but can still participate in Green Steel certification and CBAM reporting.

🚢 How CBAM affects SLRM even without direct EU exports
SLRM's alloy steel goes into automotive components, bearings, and engineering parts — many of which are exported to Europe by your OEM customers. When CBAM extends to downstream steel products (European Commission plans for 2026–27), your OEM customers will need to prove the embedded carbon in the steel they used. They will ask SLRM for this number.

Current CBAM price: €75.36/tCO₂ · Your 1.92 tCO₂e/tfs × current price = €144.7/tonne of steel CBAM cost for EU importers using Indian industry average values (2.6 tCO₂e/tfs = €196/t). Your proven 4-star Green Steel number reduces this by €51/tonne — a significant commercial advantage.
