Popular topics
Start here
1
Set up your Organisation Profile
Enter your company details, reporting boundary, and baseline year under Administration → Organisation. This drives scope assignments and framework eligibility.
2
Add & ingest emissions data
Use Data Management → Add Data or Data Collection Forms to log Scope 1, 2, and 3 activities. Import bulk CSVs via Import/Export for large datasets.
3
Review your Dashboard
The Sustainability Dashboard shows live tCO₂e totals by scope, trend charts, and data quality scores. Use filters to drill into specific periods or regions.
4
Select compliance frameworks
Go to Compliance Mapper and pick from BRSR, GreenCo, CBAM, GRI, CDP, and more. TerraCAP maps your data to each framework automatically.
5
Generate reports & plan NetZero
Export board-ready ESG reports from Reports & Compliance. Use NetZero Pathfinder to chart your trajectory to India's 2070 target with financial modeling.
