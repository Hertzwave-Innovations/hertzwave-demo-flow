Understanding Verification Standards

i
Why Standards MatterVerification standards ensure credits represent real, additional, and permanent emission reductions. Without third-party verification, credits have no regulatory or market credibility. SEBI BRSR, CSRD, and CDP all require credits from recognized standards.
Verra VCS
The Verified Carbon Standard — world's most widely used voluntary credit standard. Covers forestry (REDD+), renewable energy, agriculture, and blue carbon. Credits traded on Verra's registry, accepted by BRSR and CDP.
Gold Standard
Founded by WWF. Certifies projects delivering verified carbon reductions AND measurable SDG co-benefits. Known for rigorous stakeholder consultation. Strong for renewable energy, cookstove, and water projects.
ICVCM CCP
Integrity Council's Core Carbon Principles — highest integrity threshold for additionality, permanence, and quantification. CCP-labelled credits command a premium and are increasingly required by institutional buyers.

ℹ️
What is credit retirement? Retiring a carbon credit permanently removes it from circulation and links it to your emissions for a specific period. Once retired, credits cannot be traded, transferred, or reused — they are marked as "retired" on the issuing registry with a unique serial number. When does it happen? Retirement is executed immediately upon confirmation in Step 4. The registry is updated in real-time, a certificate is generated, and credits are removed from your active inventory. This is irreversible and required to make legitimate neutrality claims under BRSR, CSRD, and ISSB frameworks.

i
Selecting CreditsChoose which credits to retire and volume from each. Best practice: retire older vintages first (FIFO) to avoid expiry. Mix types (removal + avoidance) for a more defensible claim. Partial retirement is supported.
Tip: Include some removal credits alongside avoidance for stronger claims.
