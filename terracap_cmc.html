Which markets do you export to?
EU CBAM and UK CBAM are separate legal instruments. Exporting to Germany triggers EU CBAM obligations; exporting to the UK triggers UK CBAM. Both can apply simultaneously and require separate supplier packages.
Step 4 of 4 · Destination-market gate — EU and UK are separate CBAM instruments
ℹ
EU CBAM ≠ UK CBAM. The EU and UK CBAMs share similar design intent but are separate regulations, with different national authorities, different covered goods lists (which may diverge over time), different certificate markets, and different evidence and verification requirements. TerraCAP manages them as separate modules.
🇪🇺 European Union — EU CBAM (active Jan 2026)
Legal basis: EU Reg 2023/956 · Authority: European Commission / national customs · Annual declaration · EU ETS certificate price
🇬🇧 United Kingdom — UK CBAM (active Jan 2026)
Legal basis: UK Finance Act 2024 · Authority: HMRC · Annual declaration · UK ETS carbon price · Separate from EU CBAM
