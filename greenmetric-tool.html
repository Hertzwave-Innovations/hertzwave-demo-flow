January – April
Internal Data Collection — Gather data from Facilities, Transport, Academic Affairs, Finance, and Sustainability departments. Use TerraCAP data collection forms for each of the 6 categories.
May
GreenMetric Questionnaire Opens — UI GreenMetric opens the online questionnaire portal. Begin entering data from your TerraCAP assessment.
May – August
Data Entry & Evidence Upload — Enter all 39 indicators into the GreenMetric questionnaire. Upload evidence documents (photos, reports, policies) tagged to each indicator in TerraCAP.
September
Internal Review & QA — Cross-verify data across departments. Run TerraCAP gap analysis to identify last-minute improvements. Review evidence completeness.
October 31
Submission Deadline — Final submission of questionnaire and evidence to UI GreenMetric. Export submission-ready data package from TerraCAP.
November
Review Period — UI GreenMetric reviewers evaluate submissions. May request clarifications or additional evidence.
December
Results Published — Rankings announced globally. Compare your results with TerraCAP predictions and plan next year's improvement strategy.
Data Collection Checklist
Evidence Requirements ? UI GreenMetric requires evidence documents for most indicators since 2023. Evidence can include photos, policy documents, reports, certificates, and audit records.

Each indicator requires supporting evidence. Common evidence types include:

📐 Setting & Infrastructure
Campus maps, satellite images, zoning plans, budget allocation documents
⚡ Energy & Climate
Electricity bills, solar installation records, energy audit reports, GHG inventory
♻️ Waste
Waste audit reports, recycling MoUs, compost facility photos, hazardous waste manifests
💧 Water
Water bills, rainwater harvesting data, STP reports, water quality test results
🚌 Transportation
Vehicle registry, shuttle timetables, parking data, EV charging station records, bike-share logs
📚 Education & Research
Course catalogs, research grant records, publication lists, event photos, sustainability website URLs
