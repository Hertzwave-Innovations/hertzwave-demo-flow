Popular topics
Start here
1
Set up your Organisation Profile
Enter your company details, reporting boundary, and baseline year under Facility → Organization Profile. This drives scope assignments and framework eligibility.
2
Add & ingest emissions data
Use Data Management → Data Collection Forms to log Scope 1, 2, and 3 activities along with energy, water, waste and other ESG data. Import bulk CSVs via Data Management → Import / Export for large datasets.
3
Review your Dashboard
The Sustainability Dashboard shows live tCO₂e totals by scope, trend charts, and data quality scores. Use filters to drill into specific periods or regions.
4
Select compliance frameworks
Go to Compliance → Frameworks and pick from BRSR, CBAM, GRI, CDP, and more. TerraCAP maps your data to each framework automatically.
5
Generate reports & plan NetZero
Export board-ready ESG reports from Reports → Sustainability Report or Reports → AI Compliance Reports. Use Planning → NetZero Pathfinder to chart your trajectory to India's 2070 target with financial modeling.
6
Run ratings and assessments in Tools
Plant and campus ratings sit under Tools: GreenCo Readiness, GreenMetric, NAAC Green Audit, CII NZOC, LCA and more. They read the data you have already entered, so nothing is keyed in twice. See Tool Guides.

Tools
GreenCo Readiness

Prepares a plant for the CII GreenCo rating. GreenCo rates one plant at a time on points: under Version 3, manufacturing plants are scored on 10 parameters out of 1000, and service facilities out of 825. Open it from Tools → GreenCo Readiness.

1
Plant profile
Choose the plant, sector and scoring version. The sector sets the energy, renewable and GHG thresholds that apply; override a classification only with a reason the assessor will accept.
2
Plant data
Values already in TerraCAP, such as energy, Scope 1, 2 and 3, water and waste, fill in from the ledger and are locked. Enter only what is missing. To use a different value for GreenCo, override the field and give a reason; the ledger itself does not change.
3
Assessment
Performance credits score themselves from plant data. For governance and system credits, set how far each is in place; the assessor verifies these against evidence. Tick each mandatory requirement once it is met.
4
Initiatives
Record each parameter's initiatives with the yearly quantities they claim. The tool shows whether they reach the target band and what is still needed. AI review then checks whether those quantities are realistic and suggests how to tune them. AI never changes a score.
5
Gaps and roadmap
See where points are lost, what would move each credit, and how the planned initiatives step the score up band by band.
6
Evidence
Records already ingested into TerraCAP, such as bills, invoices and waste manifests, link automatically to the credits their data feeds. Upload the documents that exist only for GreenCo, such as policies, review minutes and project reports.
7
Submission pack
Print or export the readiness pack, with the credit table, initiatives register and data sources. It is in TerraCAP format; map it to the CII questionnaire for the version the plant registers under.
Scores are indicative. They are not a CII rating; CII assessors and the judges' panel determine the actual rating. Version 3 is scored against CII's published credit rules. Version 4 and GreenCo Lite scores are provisional, because CII has published their parameter weights but not their credit criteria.

Other tools
GreenMetric Tool
Tools → GreenMetric Tool
Self-assessment for the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings. Set up the campus profile, score each indicator, review gaps, attach evidence and prepare the submission. Covers the 2025 and 2026 questionnaires.
NAAC Green Audit
Tools → NAAC Green Audit
Green audit workflow for higher-education institutions preparing for NAAC accreditation.
CII NZOC Tool
Tools → CII NZOC Tool (opens in a new tab)
Readiness for CII's Net Zero Operational Carbon certification, which is awarded to facilities that bring Scope 1 and 2 emissions to zero through energy efficiency, renewable energy and credible offsets.
LCA Tool
Tools → LCA Tool
Life cycle assessment of products and processes.
