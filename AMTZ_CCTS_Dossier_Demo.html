What this dossier is and what it does:
CCTS India requires any entity registering carbon credits to submit a formal project dossier showing (a) what activity generated the emission reductions, (b) how the reductions were measured, and (c) that the credits have not been claimed anywhere else. This dossier covers AMTZ's zone-level activities for FY 2023–24. Credits registered here can be traded on the Indian Carbon Market (ICM) or retired to offset the carbon footprint of AMTZ or its tenants.

Section 2

Project Description

AMTZ undertook two distinct emission reduction activities during FY 2023–24, both within the zone's common infrastructure boundary under its operational control.

Activity 1 — Rooftop Solar Installation (1.2 MWp)

AMTZ commissioned a 1.2 megawatt-peak (MWp) rooftop solar photovoltaic system across Zone Blocks A, B, and C during Q2 FY 2023–24. The system was installed by Tata Power Solar Systems Ltd. and commissioned on 18 August 2023. The solar output feeds the zone's common area infrastructure (lighting, HVAC, DISCOM meters for shared services) under an intra-zone energy sharing arrangement approved by APERC.

Activity 2 — LED Lighting + HVAC Optimisation

AMTZ replaced all sodium vapour and metal halide fixtures in common areas, streets, and car parks with LED luminaires (Phase 1: 2,840 fittings). Simultaneously, the zone's central HVAC plant serving shared corridors and the Zone Admin Building was retrofitted with variable frequency drives (VFDs) on all chiller compressors. Both measures were implemented between April and June 2023.

Why these two activities qualify under CCTS:
CCTS India recognises emission reductions from (i) renewable energy generation displacing grid electricity, and (ii) energy efficiency improvements in industrial and commercial settings. Both activities result in a measurable, verifiable reduction in grid electricity consumption, which reduces the CO₂ emissions associated with AP-TRANSCO power supply to the zone. The emission factor used is the CEA national/state grid emission factor for FY 2023–24, as mandated by BEE CCTS methodology.

What AMTZ Needs to Do Next

This section lays out the concrete actions AMTZ Zone Authority needs to take to maximise value from its CCTS sub-registrar status and progress to the next crediting cycle.

5.1 Immediate Actions (within 60 days)

⚡ Trade or retire the 920 available credits
1
Contact BEE-empanelled brokers or access the ICM exchange portal (when live) to list the 920 available credits for sale. Alternatively, offer credits to zone tenants at a negotiated price for their own Scope 2 offset disclosures. Revenue from credit sales can offset the cost of TerraCAP platform fees and future efficiency investments.
2
If any additional tenant wants to retire credits against their FY 2023–24 footprint (Aurobindo, Wipro GE, etc.) — initiate the retirement request through TerraCAP's Carbon Credits module, which connects to the BEE CCTS registry directly.
📋 Register FY 2024–25 as Series 2
3
The solar system operated for the full FY 2024–25 (vs. 7.5 months in FY 2023–24). Estimated Series 2 generation: ~1,690,000 kWh from solar alone, yielding approximately 1,208 tCO₂e — before adding the LED and HVAC savings again (~264 tCO₂e). Total Series 2 estimate: ~1,472 tCO₂e. Initiate TERI verification for FY 2024–25 data no later than June 2025 to meet the 30 September 2025 registry submission deadline.
4
TerraCAP has already captured the FY 2024–25 solar generation data (from the DISCOM API) and LED/HVAC consumption data (from smart meters). The monitoring report for Series 2 can be auto-generated from TerraCAP's Zone Data Export — share this data pack with TERI to reduce their verification time and cost.

5.2 Medium-Term Actions (FY 2025–26)

🌿 Expand the project boundary to include tenant activities
5
As zone sub-registrar, AMTZ can register emission reductions achieved by individual tenants — not just zone common infrastructure. For example, if Bharat Biotech installs an ETP heat recovery system or Divi's switches to green hydrogen, those reductions can be registered under the AMTZ sub-registrar umbrella. Each tenant would sign a Letter of Authorisation (LoA) delegating credit rights to AMTZ. This significantly expands the credit volume and positions AMTZ as a genuine carbon market aggregator for the MedTech sector.
6
Approach 3–4 tenants with high Scope 1 (process combustion) or Scope 2 (high-intensity operations) to identify feasible efficiency or fuel-switch projects. TerraCAP's NetZero Pathfinder module can model the credit-generation potential of different abatement options before investment decisions are made.
🔌 Phase 2 — BMS integration to automate monitoring data
7
Currently, solar generation and lighting/HVAC data is collected monthly from meters. Phase 2 of TerraCAP's integration roadmap connects the zone's BMS (Building Management System) via MQTT, providing real-time energy monitoring. This eliminates manual data collection for monitoring reports and reduces verification time and cost from the current ~2 weeks to approximately 3 days per year.
